Controls

Move: W, A, S, D

Aim: Mouse

Punch / Shoot: Left click

Change weapons: 1, 2, 3

Throwables: 4 or G

Last weapon: Q

Reload: R

Pick up: F or E

Heal: 7, 8, 9, 0

Goal

Loot guns and armor, stay inside the white circle as the red zone closes, and be the last one standing.

Play with friends

Online matches are hosted on a computer running the Ringfall server, so they need a host on your network.

When the host shares their address (it looks like http://192.168.1.20:8080), open it in your browser and press Play Online.

What's New!

November 14th, 2026

Big Bang

The RPG-7 is the rarest gun on the island: it turns up on about one map in ten, in military crates now and then, and in some airdrops.

Its rockets explode on impact. Keep your distance, because the blast hurts you too.

November 14th, 2026

Heavy Metal

Two tanks are parked on every map, one outside the military camp. Press F to climb in, drive with WASD and click to fire shells that burst where you aim.

The armor soaks up bullets, but the red zone still reaches you, and a wrecked tank explodes.

November 14th, 2026

Lock and Load

Empty guns now reload themselves when you pick one up, switch to it or find ammo.

This page also got a new look, and all our games now share a home page.

November 11th, 2026

Going Public

Ringfall is now playable right in your browser.

October 25th, 2026

Open Gates

The military camp can now be entered from both the north and south gates.

Special Delivery

Every time the red zone closes, a plane drops a parachuted airdrop into the safe circle: an M1 Garand, AWM-S, M249 or other top-tier gun, plus level-3 gear or an 8x scope.

Don't stand underneath it when it lands.

October 12th, 2026

Patched Up

Bandages, med kits, sodas and pills have all-new art, and you can see who's healing.
