Controls

Move: W, A, S, D

Aim: Mouse

Punch / Shoot: Left click

Change weapons: 1, 2, 3

Throwables: 4 or G

Last weapon: Q

Reload: R

Pick up: F or E

Heal: 7, 8, 9, 0

Goal

Loot guns and armor, stay inside the white circle as the red zone closes, and be the last one standing.

Play with friends

Online matches are hosted on a computer running the Ringfall server, so they need a host on your network.

When the host shares their address (it looks like http://192.168.1.20:8080), open it in your browser and press Play Online.
