D&D RPG

Controls

Move: W, A, S, D

Aim: Mouse

Punch / Shoot: Left click

Change weapons: 1, 2, 3

Throwables: 4 or G

Last weapon: Q

Reload: R

Pick up: F or E

Heal: 7, 8, 9, 0

Goal

Loot guns and armor, stay inside the white circle as the red zone closes, and be the last one standing.

In Duo and Squad you get bot teammates who follow you and can't hurt you. The last team standing wins. Blitz closes the zone twice as fast.

Play with friends

Online matches are hosted on a computer running the Ringfall server, so they need a host on your network.

When the host shares their address (it looks like http://192.168.1.20:8080), open it in your browser, pick Solo, Duo or Squad (and Blitz if you like), then press Play Online.

To team up, one of you presses Create Team and shares the code (or the Copy link link). Friends press Join Team and type it, or just open the link. Everyone with the code plays on one team.

The RPG-7 is the rarest gun on the island: it turns up on about one map in ten, in military crates now and then, and in some airdrops.

The armor soaks up bullets, but the red zone still reaches you, and a wrecked tank explodes.
